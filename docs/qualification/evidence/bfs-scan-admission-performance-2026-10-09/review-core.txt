Independent Luna review of the six core files against the frozen M5 baseline.
No reviewer source edits, tests or builds. No correctness blocker found.

Scan-leaf routing is restricted to opted-in expected level zero. Resident
validated views, including deferred dirty images, retain normal MRU access.
Miss bytes are caller-owned and receive CRC, structural, expected-level and
parent-bound checks. Inserted slots start without validation proofs. Bounds
remain traversal-local even if node-local validation succeeds first.

record_reuse=false applies to unchanged consumed cursor-copy re-descent;
initial descent, successor leaves and mutation recovery use true. Mutation,
invalidation and dirty lifecycle paths clear history via cache_note_mutation.
Admission does not advance epochs. All-dirty protection and failed-read
behavior remain unchanged. Optional history allocation failure degrades to
ordinary admission without failing cache initialization.

Corrupt reads can leave a ghost ID or an unvalidated admitted slot: neither
is a validation authority, and later reads still validate before use.
Both appended operations-table fields are source-compatible with designated
in-tree initializers after rebuilding, not binary-compatible with separately
compiled historical tables. No binary plugin compatibility is claimed here.

Performance risks: a forward scan with more absent leaves than history slots
can churn every previous identity before reuse; history lookup also costs CPU.
These require measured total reads/CRCs and matched production comparisons.
