Zero effect on release builds
=============================
The patch (hitrate-handler.patch) was applied to origin/main at b8f3e8a in a scratch
worktree and committed there as a throwaway, because `make release` requires a clean
tree. `make release` without -DBFS_HITRATE builds build/release/bfshandler.040 with
SHA-256
  e2e274bbd604dce11b2fcbcb631f0c13322034408018be72d2f6d55c99987212
which is the SHA-256 of the unpatched `reference` handler of the ExAll result cache
bound and of the published v0.3.0 68040 handler. The counter is compiled only under
BFS_HITRATE.
